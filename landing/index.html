How it works

Three things, every week.

You build the week

Lay out Monday to Friday for each child. Paste a schedule you already have and it becomes tasks. Save a week you like and apply it again later.

Your child checks in

They sign in to their own view and mark what they finished, with a note or how it felt. You see it as it happens.

The record keeps itself

Every check-in is kept. A reminder goes out each morning at the hour you choose, and a summary of the week arrives on Sunday.

Where we stand

We don't teach your children. You do.

Most homeschool software either hands you a calendar and leaves, or sells you a curriculum and takes over. This is neither.

What IME does

  • Holds the week's plan for every child
  • Keeps a record of what was actually done
  • Gives your child points, trophies and a reason to show up
  • Lets the other adults in a child's life see the same thing you do
  • Answers your child's questions with a study assistant you can switch off

What IME is not

  • Not a curriculum. You choose every subject and book
  • Not a school, and not accredited
  • No grades, no credits, no diploma
  • Not proof that you meet your local homeschool law — check with your own authority

Alongside the platform, we work with children in person: tutoring, help with schoolwork, sessions at our space, or someone who comes to you. It is arranged family by family rather than sold as a package, and you do not need to be using Independent Minds EDU to ask.
